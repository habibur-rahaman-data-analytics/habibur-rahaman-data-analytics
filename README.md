## 🔍 My Data Analytics Approach

I follow a structured, business-oriented workflow:

1. **Problem Definition** — Understand the business problem and define objectives.
2. **Data Collection** — Identify relevant datasets and extract the required information.
3. **Data Cleaning** — Handle missing values, duplicates, inconsistent formats, and data quality issues.
4. **Exploratory Data Analysis** — Examine distributions, patterns, trends, and relationships.
5. **KPI Development** — Define and calculate meaningful business performance metrics.
6. **Root Cause Analysis** — Investigate potential drivers behind observed outcomes.
7. **Visualization** — Build clear charts, reports, and interactive dashboards.
8. **Recommendations** — Translate analytical findings into practical business actions.
